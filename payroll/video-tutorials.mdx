## List of Tutorials

    
### Employee Login and Onboarding

         Know how to set up your account on RazorpayX Payroll as an employee. Log in to the Payroll Dashboard as a first time user and complete the [onboarding process](/payroll/employees#onboarding).

         
        

    
### Set Up My profile

         Know how to [set up your employee profile](/payroll/employees/my-profile#set-up-profile) on the Payroll Dashboard. Review your employment details and update the necessary information. 

         
        

    
### Mark and Modify Attendance

         Know how to [mark attendance](//payroll/employees/attendance-leaves#mark-attendance) on the Payroll Dashboard. Modify, delete and manage your attendance easily. 

         
        

    
### Apply for Leaves

         Know how you can [apply for leaves](/payroll/employees/attendance-leaves#apply-for-leaves), edit and modify leaves on the Payroll Dashboard. 

         
        

    
### Claim Reimbursements

         Know how to [claim reimbursements](/payroll/employees/reimbursements) for office expenses. 
         
         
        

    
### Declare Flexible Benefits and Upload Proofs

         Know how to declare and upload proofs for [flexible benefits](/payroll/employees/declarations/flexible-benefits) when the respective windows are open.

         
        

    
### Declare Investments and Upload Proofs

         Know how to [declare investments](/payroll/employees/declarations) during the declaration window and upload proofs when the proof upload window is open.

         
        

    
### Check Payslips and Form 16s on the Dashboard

         Know how to [check payslips](/payroll/employees/payslips-form16) and view Form 16 on the Payroll Dashboard. 

         
        

    
### Submit Resignation

         Know how to [submit your resignation](/payroll/employees/resignation) on the Payroll Dashboard. This is possible only if your organisation has enabled the resignation module.
