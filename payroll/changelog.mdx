  - **RazorpayX Changelog**: Discover new releases and updates regarding RazorpayX products.

The following table records changes made to RazorpayX Payroll since Jan 2024:

Month-Year | Feature | Description
---
April 2025 | Form 24Q | Payroll introduced check for  [accurate 24Q filing.](/payroll/tds/form-24Q)
---
March 2025 | Salary Component Library | Create [custom salary components](/payroll/component-library) for your organisation.
---
Feb 2025 | Provident Fund | Payroll has resumed automated registration and PF payments for employees. This was paused due to 2FA rule on the PF portal.
---
Dec 2024 | Tax Deductions | Create [investment declaration and proof upload windows](/payroll/tax-deductions-setup) for your organisation.
---
Nov 2024 | 2FA | Introduced [2-factor authentication](/payroll/administrator#two-factor-authentication) for the following actions: 
 - [Contractor Payments](/payroll/payroll-payouts#contractor-payments)
- [User role creation](/payroll/user-roles-workflows#create-user-roles)
- [Payroll execution](/payroll/run-payroll#execute-payroll)
- [Pay advance salary](/payroll/advance-salary#pay-advance-salary)
- [Pay reimbursements](/payroll/reimbursements#pay-reimbursements)
- [Create an employee loan](/payroll/loans#create-a-loan)
- API access
  

---
Sep 2024 | Professional Tax | Due to mandatory 2FA implementation by the Karnataka Government, [automated PT payments](/payroll/professional-tax#pt-payment-for-karnataka-employees) via Payroll for employees residing in Karnataka are temporarily paused. 
---
Aug 2024 | Provident Fund (PF) | Due to mandatory 2FA implemented by the EPFO, [automated PF payments and registration](/payroll/faqs#pf-payments) for employees via the Payroll Dashboard is temporarily paused.
---
Aug 2024 | 2FA | Introduced mandatory [2-factor authentication](/payroll/administrator#two-factor-authentication) for administrators and user roles during login when accessing the Payroll Dashboard for secure money movement.
---
Aug 2024 | Budget Changes | Revised the payroll calculations per the [changes introduced](/payroll/run-payroll) in the Union Budget for the new regime.
---
July 2024 | LWF | Introduced [enabling LWF for employees](/payroll/statutory-compliance#labour-welfare-fund-lwf) from their profiles.
---
May 2024 | Fund Loading | Introduced Source Account Validation to ensure secure fund transfer and payments.
---
May 2024 | Approval Workflow | You can set up [approval workflow](/payroll/approval-workflow) and add approvers for core payroll and salary revision actions.
