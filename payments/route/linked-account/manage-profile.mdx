## Manage Profile

You can view the business details of the Linked Account under the **Profile** tab.

To view and manage profile:
1. Log in to the Linked Account Dashboard and click **Account Settings**. The **Profile** section appears as shown below:
   
2. You can perform the following actions: Change **Password** and Change **Display Name**

## Manage Team

The admin of the Linked Account can invite other team members to view the **Dashboard** by entering the email addresses. These team members have view access except access to **Bank Details** and **Manage Team**.

To invite team members:
<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to the Linked Account Dashboard and click **Account Settings** menu from the left pane.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Go to **Manage Team** tab.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Enter the email address of the member you want to invite and click **Send Invitation**.
</Step>
</Steps>

An email is sent to the member. They should accept the invitation from their **Profile** section.
