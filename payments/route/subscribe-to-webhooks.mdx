---
title: "Subscribe to Webhooks"
description: "To subscribe to webhook events:"
---

1. Log in to the Dashboard.
2.  Navigate to **Accounts & Settings** → **Webhooks** to subscribe to any of the events mentioned in the following section.

## Webhook Events and Descriptions

Webhook Event | Description
---
`transfer.processed` | Triggered when a transfer made to a Linked Account is processed.
---
`settlement.processed` | Triggered when a transfer made to a Linked Account is settled with the parent merchant. (Available only for [Razorpay Route](/payments/route)).
---
`transfers.failed` | Triggered when a transfer made to a Linked Account is failed.

Know more about [Webhooks](/webhooks) and check the [sample payloads](/webhooks/route).
