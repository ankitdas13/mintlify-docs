## Issue a Refund

Watch this video to see how to issue refunds to your customers.

To issue a refund:
<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to the Dashboard and click **Route** under **PAYMENT PRODUCTS**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Click the **Payments** tab and then click the relevant **Payment ID**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
In the **Payment details** pane, click **Issue Refund**.
1. For a full refund, enter the total transaction amount. Enter comments if required, and then click **Issue Full Refund**.
2. For a partial refund, enter the amount. Enter comments if required, and then click **Issue Partial Refund**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
The refund transfer happens from your primary account. Hence, you must create a corresponding reversal for the Linked Account. This can be done manually, or you may use the **Reverse all Route Transfers as well** option for automatic reversal.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 5">
A dialog box is displayed. Click **Yes, Refund**.
</Step>
</Steps>
