## Steps to Search Payment Pages

To search for a Payment Page:
  
1. Log in to the Dashboard.
2. Select **Payment Pages** from the left menu and search for a payment page by specifying filters.

Filter | Description
---
Title | The title of the payment page.
---
Status | The state of the payment page. Know more about [Payment Page states](/payments/payment-pages/states#payment-pages-states-and-description/).
