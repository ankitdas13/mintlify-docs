## 1. Enable Shiprocket Option on Payment Page

<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to the Dashboard and navigate to Payment Pages.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Click the **+ Create Payment Page** button.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Click **Select Payment page**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Click **Page Settings**.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 5">
Scroll down to the **Create orders on Shiprocket** section and click **Enable**.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 6">
A pop-up will appear informing you that the following fields will be added to your Payment Page. Click **Continue**.
- Name
- Address 
- City
- State
- Pincode

</Step>
<Step title="Step 7">
Continue with the creation of your Payment Page. Know more about how to [Create a Payment Page](/payments/payment-pages/create#step-2-add-page-details/).
</Step>
</Steps>

## 2. Configure Your Shiprocket Account

<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to your [Shiprocket Account](https://app.shiprocket.in/login) or create one if you have not already.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
On your Shiprocket Dashboard:
1. Navigate to **All Channels** and click **+ Add New Channel**.

2. Navigate to Razorpay Payment Pages and click **Integrate**.
3. Click **Connect with Razorpay**. You will be redirected to the Razorpay login page. 
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Log in to your Razorpay Account.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
On the **Authorize Shiprocket** page, click **Authorize** to verify your account integration with Shiprocket.

</Step>
</Steps>
After this, you will be redirected to the Shiprocket channel page. Here you can edit your Razorpay channel as per your preferences.
