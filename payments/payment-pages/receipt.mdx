## Set Up Payment Receipt

Payment Page Receipts can be generated and shared using **Automated Rececipts** or **Manual Receipts**.

You can automatically share the payment receipt with customers via email and SMS. An auto-generated reference number is added by Razorpay.

To configure automated Payment Page receipts:
<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
While creating or editing the Payment Page, select **Payment Receipts** from the top menu ribbon.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
On the **Payment Receipts Settings** pop-up page, select **Send Automated Receipts**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
To show an input field such as Name, Address and its associated value in the receipt:
1. Enable the **Show an Input Field on Receipt** option.
2. In the drop-down list, select one of the custom input fields such as `Name`, `Address` or `Landmark`, used on the Payment Page. For example, if you have selected `Name`, the customer's name `Gaurav Kumar` will appear on the Payment Page receipt.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Click **Save**.
</Step>
</Steps>

You can also manually add a reference number to the receipt and share it with your customers.

To configure manual Payment Page receipt:

<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
On the Payment Page creation page, select **Payment Receipts** from the top menu ribbon.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
On the **Payment Receipts Settings** pop-up page, select **Send Manual Receipts**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
To show an input field such as `Name`, `Address` and its associated value in the receipt:
1. Enable the **Show an Input Field on Receipt** option.
2. In the drop-down list, select one of the custom input fields such as `Name`, `Address` or `Landmark`, used on the Payment Page. For example, if you selected `Name`, the customer's name `Gaurav Kumar` will appear on the Payment Page receipt.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Click **Save**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 5">
Navigate to the page's **Transactions Details** page. All the payments made using the Payment Page are listed here.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 6">
Click the Payment id to view the payment details.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 7">
In the **Payment Receipt** field, click the **Send Receipt** button.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 8">
Enter a reference number for the receipt as per your business requirements.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 9">
Click **Send**.
</Step>
</Steps>

You can also download the payment receipt using the **Download Receipt** button.

### Resend and Download Payment Receipt

To resend the receipt to a customer:

<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Navigate to the page's **Transactions Details** screen. All the payments made using the Payment Page are listed here.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Click on the Payment id to view the payment details.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
In the **Payment Receipt** field, click the **Send** button. This will resend the receipt to the customer.
</Step>
</Steps>

You can download the payment receipt using the **Download** button.

### Email Notification to Customers

After your customers complete the payment using the Payment Page, the payment receipt is sent to them via email as a PDF attachment. The details entered by the customer on the Payment Page appear on the email body.
