---

## View Business Details

To view business details:

<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to the Dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Click **Account & Settings** and navigate to **Business details** under **Business Settings**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
The following information is listed:
- **Business Name**
- **Business Type**
- **Registration Date**
- **Registered By**
</Step>
</Steps>
