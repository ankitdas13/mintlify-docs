## View Account Activation Details

To view account activation details:

<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to the Dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Click **Account & Settings** and navigate to **Activation details** under **Business Settings**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Click View Activation Form to view the details you had submitted at the time of KYC verification. In case you want any changes to be made to these details, contact [Razorpay Support](https://razorpay.com/support/).
</Step>
</Steps>

You can also view details such as account activated on date and account access status on this page.
