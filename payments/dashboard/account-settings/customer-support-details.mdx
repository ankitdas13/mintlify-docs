title: "Customer Support Details"
description: "You can add your support details to the transaction emails sent to your customers. This will help your customers know how to reach you for any queries."
---

## Add Support Details

To add support details:
<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to the Dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Click **Account & Settings** and navigate to **Customer support details** under **Business settings** section. 

</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Click **Add** and enter:
- Phone Number
- Email
- Website/ Contact Us Link
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Click **Submit**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Edit Support Details

To edit support details:
<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to the Dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Click **Account & Settings** and navigate to **Customer support details** under **Business settings** section. 
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Navigate to the phone number, email or website/contact us link field and click **Edit**.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Click **Submit**.
</Step>
</Steps>
