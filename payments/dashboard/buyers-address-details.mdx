---
title: "Add Buyer's Address"
description: "You should provide the buyer's address details according to the invoice copy raised to initiate the settlement. The following information is needed:"
---

- Name of Business
- Address
- City
- Postal Code
- Country
- State

<Warning>

**Watch Out!**

Without a valid invoice/airway bill and buyer's address details:

- The settlement is put on hold.
- The payment is not captured and is not included in the settlement.
The payment is settled after you share a valid invoice/airway bill and buyer's address details.

</Warning>
## Add a Buyer's Address on the Dashboard

Follow these steps to add and share the buyer's address of all the payments collected via Dashboard:

<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to the Dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Navigate to the **Transactions** tab.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Click the **Invoices** tab.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Under the **Actions** tab, click **+Add Buyer Address**. Add the buyer's address for all the payments to capture the transactions.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 5">
Fill in the following details per your invoice copy. Click **Close**.

</Step>
</Steps>
