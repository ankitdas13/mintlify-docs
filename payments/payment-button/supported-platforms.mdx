## Wix

Watch this video to see how to embed a Payment Button on your Wix site:

To embed a Payment Button on your Wix site:
<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to your Wix site and switch to Editor mode.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Navigate to **Add** → **Embed** → **Custom Embeds**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Select **HTML iframe**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Click **Enter Code** and select **Code**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 5">
Paste the Button code that you copied from the Dashboard and click **Apply**. The Payment Button appears. You can drag and place it wherever required.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 6">
Publish the page to view the changes.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Weebly

Watch this video to see how to embed a Payment Button on your Weebly site:

To embed a Payment Button on your Weebly site:
<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to your Weebly site and switch to Editor mode.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Navigate to the page on which you want to place the button.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Navigate to **Build** → **Individual Elements** → **Embed Code**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Drag and drop the element and select **Click to set Custom HTML** box.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 5">
Select the position and click **Edit Custom HTML**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 6">
Paste the Button code that you copied from the Dashboard and click out of the box. This saves the code.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 7">
Publish the page to view the changes.
</Step>
</Steps>

## GoDaddy

Watch this video to see how to embed a Payment Button on your GoDaddy site:

To embed a Payment Button on your GoDaddy site:
<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to your GoDaddy website and switch to Editor mode.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Navigate to the page on which you want to place the button.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Add a section, select **HTML** and click **Add**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Paste the Button code that you copied from the Dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 5">
Add a pixel length of `700` in **Forced Height** field.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 6">
Click **Done**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 7">
Publish the page to view the changes.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Drupal

To embed a Payment Button on your Drupal Website:

<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Download the plugin from the [Drupal](https://www.drupal.org/project/payment_button_drupal_plugin) Website.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Go to Extend from the Drupal admin menu.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Enable Razorpay Payment Button Plugin module in the Drupal admin.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Configure your What You See Is What You Get (WYSIWYG) toolbar to include the button.
</Step>
</Steps>
