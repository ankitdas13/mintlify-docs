---
title: "Supported Platforms"
description: "You can embed Razorpay Subscription Button on custom-built websites as well as those built on popular platforms such as Wix, Weebly, Blogger and Google Sites."
---

Here is a walkthrough on how to embed the Razorpay Subscription Button on these platforms.

## Wix

Follow these steps to embed the Subscription Button on your Wix site:
<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to your Wix site and switch to Editor mode.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Navigate to **Add** → **Embed** → **Custom Embeds**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Select **HTML iframe**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Click **Enter Code** and select **Code**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 5">
Paste the Button code you had copied from the Dashboard and click **Apply**. The Subscription Button appears. You can drag and place it wherever required.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 6">
Publish the page to view the changes.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Blogger

Follow these steps to embed the Subscription Button on your Blogger site:
<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to your Blogger site and navigate to Editor mode.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Navigate to the page on which you want to place the button.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Switch to **HTML view**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Paste the Button code you had copied from the Dashboard and click **Update**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 5">
Publish the page to view the changes.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Weebly

Follow these steps to embed the Subscription Button on your Weebly site:
<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to your Weebly site and switch to Editor mode.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Navigate to the page on which you want to place the button.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Navigate to **Build** → **Individual Elements** → **Embed Code**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Drag and drop the element and select **Click to set Custom HTML** box.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 5">
Select the position and click **Edit Custom HTML**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 6">
Paste the Button code you had copied from the Dashboard and click out of the box. This saves the code.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 7">
Publish the page to view the changes.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Google Sites

Follow these steps to embed the Subscription Button on your Google Site:
<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to your Google Site and switch to Editor mode.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Navigate to the page on which you want to place the button.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Navigate to **Insert** → **Embed** → **Embed Code**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Paste the Button code you had copied from the Dashboard and click **Next**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 5">
Click **Insert**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 6">
Drag and drop the element wherever required.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 7">
Publish the page to view the changes.
</Step>
</Steps>

## GoDaddy

Follow these steps to embed the Payment Button on your GoDaddy website:
<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to your GoDaddy website and switch to Editor mode.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Navigate to the page on which you want to place the button.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Add a section, select **HTML** and click **Add**.  
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Paste the Button code you had copied from the Dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 5">
Add a pixel length of `700` in **Forced Height** field.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 6">
Click **Done**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 7">
Publish the page to view the changes.
</Step>
</Steps>
