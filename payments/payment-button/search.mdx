---
title: "Search a Payment Button"
description: "You can search for a Payment Button on the Dashboard by specifying various filters."
---

To search for a Payment Button:

  
1. Log in to the Dashboard.
2. Select **Payment Button** from the left menu and search for a payment button under **Payment Button** by specifying filters.

Filter | Description
---
Title | The title of the payment button.
---
Status | The state of the payment button. Know more about [Payment Button states](/payments/payment-button/states).
