---
title: "Handle Payment Exceptions with Rainy Day Kit"
description: "While Razorpay strives to provide a positive payment experience to every customer, they might face payment exceptions such as:"
---

- Late Authorization
- Payment Downtime
- Payment Errors
To overcome such exceptions and provide a smooth payment experience to your customers, use the Rainy Day Kit.

Exception | Solution | What it does
---
Amount debited though the payment has failed | [Payment Capture Settings](/payments/payment-gateway/rainy-day/capture-settings) | Allows you to better deal with 'Money debited but Payment Failed' scenarios.
---
No communication of payment method unavailability | [Payment Downtime Notifications](/payments/payment-gateway/rainy-day/downtime) | Sends instant alerts when a particular payment method is down.
---
Errors during the payment process | [Payment Error Codes](/payments/payment-gateway/rainy-day/errors) | Helps you identify the exact cause of payment error.
