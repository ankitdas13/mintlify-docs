## Installation and Configuration

<Info>

**Handy Tips**

If you lose all your contacts or misplace your mobile phone, you can recover access to the WhatsApp bot by following this process: 
Send a text message saying `Create 100` to this phone number: `+91 6364900106`. Please ensure you send this message from your number registered on WhatsApp.

<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to the Dashboard and navigate to the **App Store**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Select the **Payment Links Bot**.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Click **Install**.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
You will receive a welcome message on the mobile number which is associated with your Razorpay Admin account.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 5">
To create a Payment Link, Send `Create `. Eg. Send `Create 100` to create a Payment Link of INR 100.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 6">
You will receive the Payment Link from Razorpay which can now be shared with your customers.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Info>
