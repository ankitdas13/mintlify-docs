## Resend Payment Links From Dashboard

Watch this video to see how to resend Payment Links to your customers.

To resend a Payment Link:
1. Log in to the Dashboard.
1. Navigate to **Payment Links**.
1. Select the Payment Link which you wish to resend. The details appear on the right-side pane.
1. Click the resend icon.
    
    
    
1. Select the modes (Email or SMS or both) in which you want to resend the Payment Link.
    
    
    
1. Click **Send Link**.

## Resend Payment Links Using API
You can resend a Payment Link in `issued` state using [this](/api/payments/payment-links/resend) API.
