<Warning>

**Watch Out!**

Razorpay supports wallet auto-debit only for **Paytm**.

</Warning>
## Prerequisites
<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
You need to have an active Razorpay account with Optimizer enabled. 
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
You need to have an active Paytm PG account.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Write to your Razorpay Relationship Manager to enable Wallet Auto-Debit feature on your Optimizer account.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Write to your Paytm Relationship Manager to enable the Paytm wallet auto debit feature and provide the client key and secret.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Info>

**Handy Tips**

The Paytm auto-debit feature is supported on Razorpay Standard Checkout.

</Info>
## Add Paytm as a Payment Provider
<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to your Dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Go to the **PAYMENT PRODUCTS** section and click **Optimizer**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
In the top-right section, click **Add Provider**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Select **PayTm** in the list of gateways available and click **Next**.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 5">
Select **Server-to-Server** and click **Next**.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 6">
Enter the provider name and description and click **Next**.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 7">
Enter your **INDUSTRY_TYPE_ID**, **KEY**, **MID** (merchant ID) and **WEBSITE** details.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 8">
Enable the **Wallet auto-debit** button, enter your **Client Key and Secret** and click **Submit**.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Razorpay Wallet Auto-Debit (Customer Flow)
Below is a complete end-to-end flow about how Razorpay Wallet Auto-Debit works:

1. The customer clicks Pay Now on your website and is redirected to the Razorpay Standard Checkout.
     

2. The customer selects **Wallet** and **Paytm**.
     

    - If the customer is a first-time user, they will be asked to enter the mobile number and the OTP to link their wallet and complete the payment.
         
    - If the customer has sufficient balance in their wallet, they can pay directly with a single click.
         
    - If the customer does not have sufficient balance in their wallet, they will be redirected to a Paytm page where they can add money to their balance and complete the payment.
