## Edit Rule Details, Conditions and Target Payment Provider

To modify the rule details, conditions and target payment providers:
<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
In the list of Custom Rules, click on the required rule name.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
In the side panel, click **Edit Rule**.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
The Edit Rule screen is displayed. You can edit the rule details, conditions and target payment providers.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Click **Publish Edits** to update the rule.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Edit Rule Priority

To modify the rule priority order using the drag-and-drop:
<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
In the list of Custom Rules, click on the required rule name.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
In the side panel, click **Change Priority**.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Drag and drop the custom rule as per your business need.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Edit Rule Status

You can deactivate or publish a rule.

### Deactivate Rule
To deactivate a rule:
<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
In the list of Custom Rules, click on the required rule name.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
In the side panel, click **Deactivate Rule**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Confirm the change by clicking **Yes, Deactivate**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Once the rule is deactivated, its status changes to `Draft`.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Publish Rule
To publish a rule:
<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
In the list of Custom Rules, click on the required rule name.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
In the side panel, click **Publish Rule**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Confirm the change by clicking **Yes, Publish**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Once the rule is published, its status changes to `Live`. You can then drag-and-drop the rule to change its priority level as needed.
</Step>
</Steps>
