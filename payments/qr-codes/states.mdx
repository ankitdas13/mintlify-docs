## QR Codes States and Descriptions

The table below lists the various states of a QR Code and gives a brief description of each state:

Status | Description
---
`active` | Indicates that the QR Code has been created and is ready to accept payments. Know more about [creating a QR Code.](/payments/qr-codes/create)
---
`closed` | Indicates that the QR Code has been closed. Customers cannot make payments using a closed QR Code. Know more about [closing a QR Code.](/payments/qr-codes/close)
