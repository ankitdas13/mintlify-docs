---
title: "About Smart Collect Dashboard"
description: "Using Dashboard you can create and manage virtual bank accounts and UPI IDs tagged to specific customers."
---

To access Smart Collect:
1. Log in to Dashboard.
2. Click **Smart Collect** from the left pane.

You can perform the following actions:
- [Create Customer Identifiers](/payments/smart-collect/va-vpa-qr/dashboard/create)
- [Make Test Payments](/payments/smart-collect/va-vpa-qr/dashboard/test-payments)
- [Refund Payments](/payments/smart-collect/va-vpa-qr/dashboard/refund)
- [Close Customer Identifiers](/payments/smart-collect/va-vpa-qr/dashboard/close)
- [Search Customer Identifiers](/payments/smart-collect/va-vpa-qr/dashboard/search)
