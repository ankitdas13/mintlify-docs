---
title: "Search for a Customer Identifier"
description: "You can search for a Customer Identifier on the Dashboard by specifying various filters."
---

To search for a Customer Identifier:
  
1. Log in to the Dashboard.
2. Select **Smart Collect** from the left menu and search for a Customer Identifier under **Customer Identifiers** by specifying filters.

Filter | Description
---
Customer Identifier Id | The unique ID of the Customer Identifier.
---
Notes | The Internal Note added by you during the creation of the Customer Identifier.
