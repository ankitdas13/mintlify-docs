<Warning>

**Watch Out!**

Ensure you have a Unicommerce account with an **Admin** role and Shopify access. If you do not have access, follow [these steps](https://documentation.unicommerce.com/docs/faq-uniware.html#5-how-to-give-facility-access-to-a-user) to set it up.

</Warning>
## Integration Steps

Follow these steps on the Razorpay Dashboard: 

<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to the Dashboard and navigate to **Magic Checkout**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Select the platform of your e-commerce website, enter the relevant details and click **Next**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
In the **RTO Reduction Setup** section, navigate to the → **Delivery Tracking** tab. 
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Click **Connect** next to **Unicommerce**.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 5">
Enter the same **Username** and **Password** as your Unicommerce admin account login credentials.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 6">
Enter **Tenant** from the Unicommerce domain URL. For example, if the Unicommerce Dashboard URL after you log in is `acmecorp.unicommerce.com`, **acmecorp** is the tenant.

</Step>
</Steps>

You have successfully integrated Magic Checkout with Unicommerce. In case you want to disconnect Unicommerce, click **Disconnect**.
