## Integration Steps

Follow these steps on the Razorpay Dashboard: 

<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to the Dashboard and navigate to **Magic Checkout**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Select the platform of your e-commerce website, enter the relevant details and click **Next**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
In the **RTO Reduction Setup** section, navigate to the → **Delivery Tracking** tab. 
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Click **Connect** next to **Delhivery**.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 5">
Send an email to the **Delhivery team** on `de.onb@delhivery.com` requesting for the **Production Authentication Token**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 6">
Enter the token once you receive it and click **Connect**.

</Step>
</Steps>

You have successfully integrated Magic Checkout with Delhivery. In case you want to disconnect Delhivery, click **Disconnect**.
