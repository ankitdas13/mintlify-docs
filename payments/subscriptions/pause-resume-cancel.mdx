You can [pause](/payments/subscriptions/pause-resume-cancel#pause-a-subscription-using-dashboard), [resume](/payments/subscriptions/pause-resume-cancel#resume-a-subscription-using-dashboard) and [cancel](/payments/subscriptions/pause-resume-cancel#cancel-a-subscription-using-dashboard) a subscription from the Dashboard.

## Pause a Subscription Using Dashboard

To pause a subscription from your Dashboard:

<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to the Dashboard and click **Subscriptions** under **PAYMENT PRODUCTS** in the left menu.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Click the **Subscription Id** you want to pause.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Click **Pause Subscription**. The subscription is paused immediately.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Click **Yes, Pause**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Resume a Subscription Using Dashboard

To resume a subscription from your Dashboard:

<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to the Dashboard and click **Subscriptions** under **PAYMENT PRODUCTS** in the left menu.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Click the **Subscription Id** you want to resume.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Click **Resume Subscription**. The subscription is resumed immediately.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Click **Yes, Resume**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Cancel a Subscription Using Dashboard

<Info>

**Hand Tips**

You can either cancel the subscription immediately or at the end of the current billing cycle.

Watch the below video to know how to pause, resume and cancel a Subscription from the Dashboard.

To cancel a subscription from your Dashboard:

<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to the Dashboard and click **Subscriptions** under **PAYMENT PRODUCTS** in the left menu.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Click the **Subscription Id** you want to cancel.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Click **Cancel Subscription**. You can choose to either cancel the subscription immediately or at the end of the current billing cycle.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Click **Yes, Cancel**.
</Step>
</Steps>

</Info>
