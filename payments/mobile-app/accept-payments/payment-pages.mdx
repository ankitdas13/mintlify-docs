Create Razorpay Payment Pages with ready-to-use templates and collect payments.

## Create Payment Page

Follow these steps to create a Payment Page:

<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in with your Dashboard credentials.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Tap **Payment Pages** under the **Accept Payments** section.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Tap **+ Create Payment Page** and select the template of your choice.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Add business details such as page title, description, contact information and so on. Know more about how to add [business details](/payments/payment-pages/create#add-business-details).
</Step>
<Step title="Step 5">
You can also add a goal tracker to track your goals. To add a goal tracker, tap **+ Add a Goal Tracker**. Know more about how to [configure a goal tracker](/payments/payment-pages/goal-tracker).

</Step>
<Step title="Step 6">
Add payment details such as price field, email, phone number and so on. Know more about how to add [payment details](/payments/payment-pages/create#add-payment-details). Tap **Publish Page**.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Share Payment Pages

You can share the Payment Pages using:
- SMS and Email
- Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and more.

## Edit Payment Page

Follow these steps to edit a Payment Page:

<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Under the **Accept Payments** section, tap **Payment Pages** and scroll down to the list of Payment Pages. 

</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Tap on the payment page you wish to edit.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Tap **Edit Page**.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
The page appears in edit mode. You can now edit any of the fields to update the details, including the price fields.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Deactivate Payment Page

Follow these steps to deactivate a Payment Page:

<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Under the **Accept Payments** section, tap **Payment Pages** and scroll down to the list of Payment Pages. 

</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Tap on the payment page you wish to deactivate.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Tap **Deactivate**.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Tap **Yes, deactivate** to confirm.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Payment Page States

The table below lists the various states in the Payment Page life cycle and briefly describes each.

Status | Description 
---
Active | Indicates the Payment Page is created and saved. You can accept payments using this page and edit the page. 
---
Inactive | Indicates the Payment Page is deactivated or has expired. You cannot accept payments using this page.
