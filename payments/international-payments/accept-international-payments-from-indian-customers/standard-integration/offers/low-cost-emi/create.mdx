---
title: "Create Low Cost EMI Offers"
description: "A Low Cost EMI is an offer where you can decide the cost to subvent for each EMI tenure and the customer bears the remaining cost."
---

This approach ensures that customers enjoy the benefits of EMI at an affordable price, helping you minimise the overall cost.
**Handy Tips**

Once you create a Low Cost EMI offer, you cannot edit it. To make changes, disable the previous offer and create a new one. 

## Steps to Create Low Cost EMI Offers 

Watch this video to see how to create an offer.

To create Low Cost EMI offers:

<Steps>
<Step title="Log in to the Dashboard.">
Log in to the Dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Navigate to **Offers** and click **+ Create No & Low Cost EMI**.">
Navigate to **Offers** and click **+ Create No & Low Cost EMI**.

</Step>
<Step title="The **Create an Offer** wizard appears with the following four sect...">
The **Create an Offer** wizard appears with the following four sections. Enter details in all these sections to create the offer.
- [**Description**](#description)
- [**Discount Type**](#discount-type)
- [**Applicable On**](#applicable-on)
- [**Offer Validity**](#offer-validity)
</Step>
</Steps>

You can review the offer configurations at the end under the [**Overview**](#overview) tab.

## Description

In the **Description** section, enter the following details. All the fields are mandatory.

<Steps>
<Step title="**Offer Name**: Enter the name of the offer. For example, **Diwali...">
**Offer Name**: Enter the name of the offer. For example, **Diwali Dhamaka**. This appears at the Checkout.
</Step>
<Step title="**Display Text**: Enter a meaningful description for the offer. For...">
**Display Text**: Enter a meaningful description for the offer. For example, **Low Cost EMI Offers**. This appears at the Checkout.
</Step>
<Step title="**Terms**: Enter the terms and conditions for this offer.">
**Terms**: Enter the terms and conditions for this offer.
</Step>
<Step title="Click **Next**.">
Click **Next**.
</Step>
</Steps>
  

## Discount Type
In the **Discount Type** section, enter the discount details that should be applied to the offer.

<Steps>
<Step title="**Minimum Order amount**: Enter the minimum bill amount for which t...">
**Minimum Order amount**: Enter the minimum bill amount for which the Low Cost EMI Offer can be applied. For example, the offer can be applied to a minimum amount of **₹4,000**. This is a mandatory field.
</Step>
<Step title="**Maximum Order amount**: Enter the maximum bill amount for which t...">
**Maximum Order amount**: Enter the maximum bill amount for which the Low Cost EMI Offer can be applied. For example, the offer can be applied to a maximum amount of **₹1,00,000**.
</Step>
<Step title="Click **Next**.">
Click **Next**.
</Step>
</Steps>
  

## Applicable On

Under the **Applicable On** tab, fill in the following details:

<Steps>
<Step title="**Issuer**: Select the bank issuing the Low Cost EMI and click **Ne...">
**Issuer**: Select the bank issuing the Low Cost EMI and click **Next**.
</Step>
<Step title="**EMI Tenure**: Select the tenures to be listed at the Checkout.">
**EMI Tenure**: Select the tenures to be listed at the Checkout.
</Step>
<Step title="**EMI Offer Type**: Select **Low Cost EMI** from the drop-down list...">
**EMI Offer Type**: Select **Low Cost EMI** from the drop-down list and configure the interest rate that you will bear for each tenure. The remaining interest rate is auto-filled which is borne by the customer.
</Step>
<Step title="Click **Next**.">
Click **Next**.
</Step>
</Steps>
  

## Offer Validity

In the **Offer Validity** tab, set how long the offer should be valid and how you want to handle the payment failure situations:

<Steps>
<Step title="**Starting On**: Select the **Starts Immediately** check box for th...">
**Starting On**: Select the **Starts Immediately** check box for the offer to come into effect immediately. Alternatively, you can select the date and time the created Offer should become active.
</Step>
<Step title="**Expires On**: Select the date and time the offer should end.">
**Expires On**: Select the date and time the offer should end.
</Step>
<Step title="**On Payment Failure**: Define how to handle payment failure.">
**On Payment Failure**: Define how to handle payment failure.
- **Do not allow payment to go through**: The payment has failed.
- **Allow customer to pay without availing offer**: The payment is allowed even though the set validations are not met. However, the offer is not applied to the bill amount. The customer will be charged the entire order amount.
</Step>
<Step title="**Max Usage**: Set the number of times the offer should be applied...">
**Max Usage**: Set the number of times the offer should be applied across all transactions. For example, **100**.
</Step>
<Step title="**Show Offer on Checkout**: Select this check box for the created o...">
**Show Offer on Checkout**: Select this check box for the created offer to be displayed for all Standard Checkout payments including [Payment Links](/payments/payment-links).
</Step>
<Step title="Click **Next**.">
Click **Next**.
</Step>
</Steps>

  

## Overview

Click the **Overview** tab to view the offer summary you just created.

1. **Terms and Conditions**: Select the check box after you have read the disclaimer.
2. Click **Create EMI Offer**.
  

By default, all the created offers are in the **enabled** state.

## Test the Low Cost EMI Offer

You can test the created Low Cost EMI offer for all Standard Checkout payments, including [Payment Links](/payments/payment-links), [Payment Button](/payments/payment-button), [Payment Pages](/payments/payment-pages) and so on.

<Info>
**Handy Tips**
</Info>

- You can toggle between Live and Test Modes on your **Dashboard**. Navigate to the top menu ribbon and click the drop-down icon against **Live Mode**. Toggle to **Test Mode** and test the offers enabled.

- You can make test payments using one of the payment methods at the Checkout. No money is deducted from your account as this is a simulated transaction.

We will test the offer using a valid Payment Link for this example. Follow the steps given below: 

<Steps>
<Step title="Select the **Payment Link Id** you wish to test the created offer f...">
Select the **Payment Link Id** you wish to test the created offer from the Dashboard.

</Step>
<Step title="Copy the **Payment Link** URL and open it in your browser.">
Copy the **Payment Link** URL and open it in your browser.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter your contact details and click **Proceed**.">
Enter your contact details and click **Proceed**.
</Step>
<Step title="Select **EMI**.">
Select **EMI**.
</Step>
<Step title="Select the payment option you created for the offer on from the Das...">
Select the payment option you created for the offer on from the Dashboard. Select the EMI plan and enter the required [test details](/payments/payments/test-card-details).
</Step>
<Step title="Click **Pay via EMI**">
Click **Pay via EMI**
</Step>
<Step title="Select **Success** or **Failure**, depending on which flow you wish...">
Select **Success** or **Failure**, depending on which flow you wish to test.
</Step>
<Step title="You should see a confirmation message depending on the flow you hav...">
You should see a confirmation message depending on the flow you have selected.
</Step>
</Steps>

On successful payment, you should have received a discount on your payment. You can verify this by navigating to the **Transactions** → **Payments** tab and viewing the payment details.
