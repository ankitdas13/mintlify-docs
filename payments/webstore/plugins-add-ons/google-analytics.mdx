**Available Tracking Metrics**

Metric | Description
---
Page View | When a customer lands on your Webstore. This is the default metric to track page visits.

## Prerequisites

[Create a Google Tracking ID](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/1008080?hl=en). 

## Add Google Tracking ID to Your Webstore

To add Google Tracking ID to your Webstore:
<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Navigate to your Webstore in the edit mode.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Click **Page Settings**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Go to **Plugins and Add-ons** and click **Configure**.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Add the **Tracking ID** to track the page views.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 5">
Click **Save**.
</Step>
</Steps>
