## Search for Webstore
You can search for a Webstore on the Dashboard by specifying various filters. 

To search for a Webstore:
  
<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to the Dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Navigate to **Payment Pages** from the left menu and select **Razorpay Webstore**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Search for the Webstore by specifying filters.
</Step>
</Steps>

Filter | Description
---
Title | The title of the store.
---
Status | The state of the store. Know more about [Webstore states](/payments/webstore/states).

## Search for Product

You can search for products added to your various stores on the Dashboard by specifying various filters. 

To search for a Product:

<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to the Dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Navigate to **Payment Pages** from the left menu and click **Products**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Search for the product by specifying filters.
</Step>
</Steps>

Filter | Description
---
Product name | The name of the product.
---
Status | The state of the product. Possible values are: -  Available 
-  Out of Stock
