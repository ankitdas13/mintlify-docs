---

## Steps

To use RTO intelligence:

<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Log in to the Dashboard. 
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Navigate to **Magic Checkout** → **Setup and Settings** → **RTO Settings**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Turn on **Enable RTO Intelligence**. This automatically disables the cash on delivery option in case of high-risk customers who often return products on delivery resulting in damaged products during transit, high returns, refund issues, and so on. 
</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Click **Enable RTO Intelligence** in the confirmation pop-up modal.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>

**Watch Out!**

To enable RTO Intelligence, you must activate Magic Checkout.

</Warning>
### To activate Magic Checkout:

1. Click **Activate** under **Enable RTO Intelligence**.

1. On the **Checkout Settings** page, turn on **Enable Magic Checkout**.
1. Click **Save Settings**.
