## Integration Steps

Follow these steps on the Razorpay Dashboard: 

<Steps>
<Step title="Log in to the Dashboard.">
Log in to the Dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Navigate to **Magic Checkout** → **Setup and Settings**.">
Navigate to **Magic Checkout** → **Setup and Settings**.
</Step>
<Step title="Navigate to **RTO Settings** → **Delivery Tracking** and click **Co...">
Navigate to **RTO Settings** → **Delivery Tracking** and click **Connect** next to **ClickPost**.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter the same **Username** and **Key** as provided by ClickPost du...">
Enter the same **Username** and **Key** as provided by ClickPost during the registration.
</Step>
<Step title="Click **Connect to ClickPost**.">
Click **Connect to ClickPost**.

</Step>
</Steps>

You have successfully integrated COD & Magic Checkout with ClickPost. In case you want to disconnect ClickPost, click **Disconnect**.
