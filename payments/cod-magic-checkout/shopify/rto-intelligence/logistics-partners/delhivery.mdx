## Integration Steps

Follow these steps on the Razorpay Dashboard: 

<Steps>
<Step title="Log in to the Dashboard.">
Log in to the Dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Navigate to **Magic Checkout** → **Setup and Settings**.">
Navigate to **Magic Checkout** → **Setup and Settings**.
</Step>
<Step title="Navigate to **RTO Settings** → **Delivery Tracking** and click **Co...">
Navigate to **RTO Settings** → **Delivery Tracking** and click **Connect** next to **Delhivery**.
</Step>
<Step title="Send an email to the Delhivery team at `de.onb@delhivery.com` reque...">
Send an email to the Delhivery team at `de.onb@delhivery.com` requesting the **Production Authentication Token**.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter the token once you receive it and click **Connect**.">
Enter the token once you receive it and click **Connect**.

</Step>
</Steps>

You have successfully integrated COD & Magic Checkout with Delhivery. In case you want to disconnect Delhivery, click **Disconnect**.
