## Payment Flow 

Customers select the products on your website or app and proceed to Checkout. On the Checkout page, the customers:

<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Enter the **Phone Number** and click **Continue**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Select **EMI** as the payment method.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Select **Credit Card**.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Select **OneCard** from the list and the EMI tenure. Click **Continue**.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 5">
Enter the OneCard Credit Card details and choose if they want to **Save this card as per RBI guidelines** or pay without saving the card.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 6">
Click **Continue**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 7">
Razorpay Checkout redirects to OneCard for approval. You can either swipe right on your OneCard app to approve payment or click **Approve using OTP** and enter the OTP sent to your mobile number.
</Step>
</Steps>

When the customers complete the payment on the Checkout, the entire transaction amount is authorized by OneCard and converted into EMIs within 3-4 days, depending on the payment terms agreed with the credit card provider.
