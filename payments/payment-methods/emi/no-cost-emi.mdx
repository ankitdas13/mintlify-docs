## Create No Cost EMI Offers

1. You can create No Cost EMIs for a selected issuer from your Dashboard. 
2. Follow the steps in Offers to [create No Cost EMIs](/payments/offers/create) on the Dashboard.

## How it Works

<Steps>
<Step title="Step 1">
Enter the **Phone Number** and click **Continue**.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 2">
Select **EMI** as the payment method.
</Step>
<Step title="Step 3">
Select the EMI option where No Cost EMI is available. If the amount entered in the Checkout is eligible for the discount, all the applicable EMI options are shown.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 4">
Select the EMI tenure and click **Continue**. They enter the relevant details.

</Step>
<Step title="Step 5">
If the customers are:
- **Eligible** to avail of the EMI, they click **Continue**. 
- **Not eligible** for availing the EMI, they have to pay the full amount. They click **Pay full amount** and are prompted to confirm the action. 
</Step>
<Step title="Step 6">
Choose if they want to **Save this card as per RBI guidelines** or pay without saving the card.
</Step>
</Steps>

After the successful payment, Razorpay redirects customers to your application or website. By the end of the EMI cycle, the card issuer deducts the EMI amount from the customer’s bank.
