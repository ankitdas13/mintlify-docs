description: "This approval workflow is based on the [cost center](/x/cost-centers) that you have linked to your invoice. You can add a cost center to your invoice at the time of creation and the workflow linked to that cost center for an invoice will become applicable to your invoice."
---

You can [contact support](/x/support) to setup this approval workflow and assign it to relevant team members.

While [creating or adding an invoice](/x/vendor-payments/invoices#add-invoices), you can **Link PO** and add a **Cost Center** in the **Invoice Details** section.

After adding the cost center when you review the final invoice details, you can hover over **Show all** at the right-bottom of your screen to view the set approval cycle. Click **Send for Approval**.

    
### Approving an Invoice

         To approve or reject an Invoice:

         1. Log in to the [RazorpayX Dashboard](https://x.razorpay.com/auth).
            
<Info>
**Handy Tips**
</Info>

You can either find the Invoices waiting for your approval on the Dashboard or follow the steps below.

         2. Navigate to **Vendor Payments** → **Invoices**.
         3. Hover over the required Invoice `In Approval` and click **APPROVE** or **REJECT** or select it to view more details and click **APPROVE** or **REJECT** on the right-pane.
            1. If you click **APPROVE**, the following screen appears. Enter comments and click **Approve**.
                
            2. If you click **REJECT**, enter the reason for rejection and click **Reject**.
        

Once your invoice is approved or rejected, you receive a mail from RazorpayX.

### View the Invoices Workflow

To view the approval workflow after the setup:

<Steps>
<Step title="Log in to the [RazorpayX Dashboard](http://x.razorpay.com/auth).">
Log in to the [RazorpayX Dashboard](http://x.razorpay.com/auth).
</Step>
<Step title="Navigate to the profile icon → **My Accounts & Settings** → **Workf...">
Navigate to the profile icon → **My Accounts & Settings** → **Workflow** → **Invoices**.
</Step>
<Step title="Click **View Workflows** and select the cost center for which you w...">
Click **View Workflows** and select the cost center for which you want to view the workflow.
</Step>
</Steps>
