## View Account Statement
You can view all the transactions at once or filter it as per your requirement.

 

## Download Account Statement
You can download all of your transaction data for a specific day, month or any time frame as per your business requirements. This information can be downloaded as a `csv` or `xls` file, or sent via email to the intended recipients.

To download account statement:

<Steps>
<Step title="Log in to the [RazorpayX Dashboard](https://x.razorpay.com/auth).">
Log in to the [RazorpayX Dashboard](https://x.razorpay.com/auth).
</Step>
<Step title="Navigate to **Account Statement** on the left navigation.">
Navigate to **Account Statement** on the left navigation.
</Step>
<Step title="Click **EXPORT**.">
Click **EXPORT**.

</Step>
<Step title="Select the date range for which you require the statement from the...">
Select the date range for which you require the statement from the drop-down.
</Step>
<Step title="Select the format in which you wish to download the statement. You...">
Select the format in which you wish to download the statement. You can choose between `csv` or `xls` files.
</Step>
<Step title="**DOWNLOAD** the statement.">
**DOWNLOAD** the statement.

</Step>
</Steps>
<Warning>

**Watch Out!**

- Generating statements can take up to 10 minutes.
- The closing balance shown on the dashboard is generated by Razorpay and may differ slightly from real-time bank data.

7. You can also **EMAIL** the statement. Select the receivers from the list and click **EMAIL**.

</Warning>
