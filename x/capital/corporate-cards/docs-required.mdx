## Document Checklist

Find the list of documents required for different business entity types.

### Private and Public Limited Companies 
#### Company KYC
<Steps>
<Step title="Company PAN Card">
Company PAN Card
</Step>
<Step title="Certificate of Incorporation (COI)">
Certificate of Incorporation (COI)
</Step>
<Step title="Memorandum of Association (MOA) & Articles of Association (AOA)">
Memorandum of Association (MOA) & Articles of Association (AOA)
</Step>
<Step title="Company’s GST Registration Certificate (operational address proof)">
Company’s GST Registration Certificate (operational address proof)
</Step>
<Step title="Company address proof: Utility bill (not older than 60 days), regis...">
Company address proof: Utility bill (not older than 60 days), registered and unexpired rent agreement or Udyam Registration.
</Step>
<Step title="Audited financials for the last 3 years + provisional financials fo...">
Audited financials for the last 3 years + provisional financials for the current year (if applicable).
</Step>
<Step title="Last 6 months' bank Statements from your company main account (espe...">
Last 6 months' bank Statements from your company main account (especially where Razorpay settlements are received).
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Directors / Authorised Signatories' KYC
<Steps>
<Step title="PAN Card">
PAN Card
</Step>
<Step title="Aadhaar, Passport or Voter id">
Aadhaar, Passport or Voter id
</Step>
<Step title="Recent Photograph">
Recent Photograph
</Step>
</Steps>

### Partnership Firms
#### Firm KYC
<Steps>
<Step title="Partnership PAN Card">
Partnership PAN Card
</Step>
<Step title="Partnership Deed">
Partnership Deed
</Step>
<Step title="GST Registration (verify your operational address)">
GST Registration (verify your operational address)
</Step>
<Step title="Current address proof: A utility bill (not older than 60 days), a r...">
Current address proof: A utility bill (not older than 60 days), a registered and unexpired rent agreement, Udyam Registration, or a Trade License/Municipal License.
</Step>
<Step title="Last 6 months' bank Statements (digital copies preferred)">
Last 6 months' bank Statements (digital copies preferred)
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Partner KYC
<Steps>

Aadhaar, Passport or Voter id
</Step>

Recent Photograph
</Step>
</Steps>

### Proprietorships
#### Proprietary Firm KYC
<Steps>
<Step title="PAN Card of the Proprietor (this acts as your business PAN)">
PAN Card of the Proprietor (this acts as your business PAN)
</Step>
<Step title="Any 2 business existence proofs : GST Certificate, Udyam Registrati...">
Any 2 business existence proofs : GST Certificate, Udyam Registration, Shop & Establishment Act License, Trade License, or an MSME Certificate
</Step>
<Step title="Business address proof: If your business does not operate at the ad...">
Business address proof: If your business does not operate at the address on the above proofs, a registered rental agreement is required.
</Step>
<Step title="Last 6 months' bank statement.">
Last 6 months' bank statement.
</Step>
</Steps>

**Handy Tips**

- Minimum business vintage required for all private and public companies, LLPs and partnership firms is 1 year; 2 years for proprietary firms.
