title: "Scheduled Payouts"
description: "Use the RazorpayX Scheduled payouts to schedule payouts to your Contact's bank account or VPA, to be processed at a later time. For example, you can use Scheduled payouts to schedule salary payouts for your employees."
---

- Your account balance is not blocked when you create a Scheduled payout.
- You can schedule as many payouts as you like irrespective of your account balance. We check your account balance at the time of processing.
- You can schedule payouts up to 3 months in advance, thus allowing you to better plan the float in your account.

<Info>
**Handy Tips**
</Info>

- This feature is only available on the RazorpayX Dashboard. It is not available on APIs.
- You cannot schedule payouts to cards.

## How it Works

You can create Scheduled payouts via the RazorpayX Dashboard or using Bulk Upload.

The process to create a Scheduled payout is similar to how you create a regular payout.
<Steps>
<Step title="Create a [Contact](/x/contacts#contacts).">
Create a [Contact](/x/contacts#contacts).
</Step>
<Step title="Create a [Fund account](/x/contacts#fund-accounts) for the Contact.">
Create a [Fund account](/x/contacts#fund-accounts) for the Contact.
</Step>
<Step title="Create a payout.">
Create a payout.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Individual Payout

You can create a Scheduled payout via the RazorpayX Dashboard. To create a Scheduled payout, select the following options while creating a payout:
<Steps>
<Step title="Select **Schedule the payout**.">
Select **Schedule the payout**.
</Step>
<Step title="Select a date when you want the payout to be processed. You can sch...">
Select a date when you want the payout to be processed. You can schedule payouts up to 3 months in advance.
</Step>
<Step title="Select a time slot when you want the payout to be processed. Follow...">
Select a time slot when you want the payout to be processed. Following are the 4 slots available:
</Step>
</Steps>
  - 9 a.m. to 10 a.m.
  - 1 p.m. to 2 p.m.
  - 5 p.m. to 6 p.m.
  - 9 p.m. to 10 p.m.

After a payout is created, it moves to the `pending` or `scheduled` state.

### Bulk Upload

You can create Scheduled payouts created using [Bulk Upload](/x/contacts/bulk-uploads). Select the following options while uploading the bulk upload file:

<Steps>
<Step title="Select **Schedule the payout**.">
Select **Schedule the payout**.
</Step>
<Step title="Select the date when you want the payout to be processed. You can s...">
Select the date when you want the payout to be processed. You can schedule payouts up to 3 months in advance.
</Step>
<Step title="Select a time slot when you want the payout to be processed. Follow...">
Select a time slot when you want the payout to be processed. Following are the 4 slots available:
</Step>
</Steps>
  - 9 a.m. to 10 a.m.
  - 1 p.m. to 2 p.m.
  - 5 p.m. to 6 p.m.
  - 9 p.m. to 10 p.m.

After a bulk file is uploaded, it is picked up for processing immediately. Know more about [bulk payouts](/x/bulk-payouts).

Payouts created via the bulk upload move to either the `pending` or `scheduled` state.

<Info>
**Handy Tips**
</Info>

You cannot use this feature to schedule when a bulk upload file is picked up for processing. Once uploaded, the bulk upload file is processed immediately. The individual payouts created are scheduled as per the selected options.

### Life Cycle

A scheduled payout can have the following statuses during its life cycle:

- `pending`
- `scheduled`
- `processing`
- `processed`
- `reversed`
- `cancelled`
- `rejected`
- `failed`

Know  more about the [states and the life cycle of a payout](/x/payouts/states-life-cycle).

<Info>
**Handy Tips**
</Info>

The `Pending` and `Rejected` states are available only if you have [Approval Workflow](/x/manage-teams/approval-workflow) enabled on your account.
