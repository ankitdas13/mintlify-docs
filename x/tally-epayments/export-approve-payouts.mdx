You can approve these in bulk and then export the approved payouts back to TallyPrime to reconcile your payments automatically. Know more about [Tally e-Payments](/x/tally-epayments).

Following is the process to move the data between TallyPrime and RazorpayX. 

## Export Data

You must first [set up a RazorpayX ledger in TallyPrime](/x/tally-epayments/set-up) and then export the CSV file data to RazorpayX.

To export the payout data you have on TallyPrime into RazorpayX:

<Steps>
<Step title="Open TallyPrime and navigate to the Gateway.">
Open TallyPrime and navigate to the Gateway.
</Step>
<Step title="Go to **Utilities** → **Banking**.">
Go to **Utilities** → **Banking**.
</Step>
<Step title="Select **e-Payments**. The e-Payments screen is displayed.">
Select **e-Payments**. The e-Payments screen is displayed.
</Step>
<Step title="Select the **Ready for sending to bank** row as shown below:">
Select the **Ready for sending to bank** row as shown below:

</Step>
<Step title="Select the transactions which you wish to export from the list.">
Select the transactions which you wish to export from the list.
</Step>
<Step title="On the top menu, select **Export** → **Payment Instructions**.">
On the top menu, select **Export** → **Payment Instructions**.
</Step>
<Step title="Click **Yes** to confirm.">
Click **Yes** to confirm.
</Step>
</Steps>

You see a message after successful completion of the data export. Then you [upload the payout data](#upload-payout-data) into the RazorpayX Dashboard for review and approval.

## RazorpayX Dashboard Actions 

Switch to your RazorpayX Dashboard to upload and approve the payouts. 

## Upload Payout Data

To upload payout data:
1. Log in to your [RazorpayX Dashboard](https://x.razorpay.com/).
1. Navigate to **Payouts** → **Tally Payouts**.
1. Click **Tally bulk import** under the **+ NEW** drop-down menu, as shown below.
   
1. Click **+ BULK PAYOUT**.
1. Upload the payouts data file exported from TallyPrime and click **Next**.
   
1. Preview the payouts and click Next.
1. Enter the OTP sent to your registered mobile number and email address and click **Confirm Payouts**.
   

Bulk payouts are created and listed on the Tally Payouts screen.

## Approve Payouts

After the payouts are uploaded to the RazorpayX Dashboard, you can access the payout data for further review and approval.
 
To approve these payouts in bulk:
<Steps>
<Step title="Go to **Payouts** → **Pending on you**.">
Go to **Payouts** → **Pending on you**.

</Step>
<Step title="All payouts pending on you are displayed in the **Payouts** page.">
All payouts pending on you are displayed in the **Payouts** page.
</Step>
<Step title="Use the check box to select the payouts you want to make.">
Use the check box to select the payouts you want to make.
</Step>
<Step title="Click **APPROVE** as shown below.">
Click **APPROVE** as shown below.

</Step>
</Steps>

RazorpayX shows a message confirming the payouts approval. You can now [view and download the payouts report](#view-payouts-and-download-reports).

## View Payouts and Download Reports

Click **VIEW PAYOUTS** to view all the processed payouts. Click **DOWNLOAD** to view information about unprocessed rows by downloading the error report.

You can then download the MIS report and [reconcile payouts in TallyPrime](/x/tally-epayments/reconcile-payouts).
