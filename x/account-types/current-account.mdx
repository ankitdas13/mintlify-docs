Current Accounts enable you to manage receivables and payables in one place and offer all standard banking features like a cheque book, debit card and account statement. 

<Info>
**Handy Tips**
</Info>

The Partner Banks for Current Account are [RBL](/x/rbl-ybl-current-account/company), [Yes Bank](/x/rbl-ybl-current-account/company), [IDFC First](/x/idfc-first/kyc) and [Axis Bank](/x/axis-current-account#current-account-opening-required-documents)) presently. [Contact Support](/x/support) to open a Current Account with the bank of your choice (subject to individual approval).

## Open a Current Account

To open a RazorpayX powered Current Account:

<Steps>
<Step title="Log in to the [RazorpayX Dashboard](https://x.razorpay.com/auth).">
Log in to the [RazorpayX Dashboard](https://x.razorpay.com/auth).
</Step>
<Step title="Go to the Current Account [application link](https://x.razorpay.com...">
Go to the Current Account [application link](https://x.razorpay.com/auth/signup/?intent=current_account). Keep your Business PAN/GSTIN readily available to make your application process quicker.
</Step>
<Step title="Provide your [Business PAN](https://razorpay.com/learn/company-perm...">
Provide your [Business PAN](https://razorpay.com/learn/company-permanent-account-number-pan-card/)/[GSTIN](https://razorpay.com/learn/what-is-gstin-all-you-know-about-your-15-digits-gstin/) to auto-fill your application.

</Step>
</Steps>

    You can also manually enter your business details. Refer to the list of [required documents](#required-documents).
4. Review the business details retrieved by the identity number you provided.
    1. Verify the address for the on-site visit and click **Open account at registered address**. You can also choose to provide a new address, if required. 
        
    2. Review the details of your account type, such as partner bank name and monthly minimum balance. Click **This works for me** to proceed with the account opening.
        
    3. Edit your signatories/declarations. 
5. Complete the OTP verification process. 

<Info>
**Handy Tips**
</Info>

You can always check the status of your application on the Dashboard.

After verifying your details, RazorpayX works to open your Current Account with the partner bank. You can access your Current Account details and avail the benefits on your RazorpayX [Dashboard](/x/dashboard).

## Add Funds

After account activation, add funds to your RazorpayX account to make payouts. You can do this by direct bank transfer via:
- [NEFT/RTGS/IMPS](#direct-bank-transfer-netbanking)
- [UPI](#direct-bank-transfers-upi-apps)

### Direct Bank Transfer: Netbanking

You can transfer funds to your RazorpayX account via a direct bank transfer from bank accounts. 

### Direct Bank Transfers: UPI Apps
To transfer funds to RazorpayX Lite from your UPI app:
<Steps>
<Step title="Log in to your UPI app.">
Log in to your UPI app.
</Step>
<Step title="Create a beneficiary using your RazorpayX account number and IFSC d...">
Create a beneficiary using your RazorpayX account number and IFSC details. You can find these details on your Dashboard → [**My Account & Settings**](/x/dashboard#my-account-settings).
</Step>
<Step title="Transfer funds via IMPS.">
Transfer funds via IMPS.
</Step>
</Steps>
