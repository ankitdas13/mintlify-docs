## Download Report

To generate the report:

<Steps>
<Step title="Log in to the [RazorpayX Dashboard](https://x.razorpay.com/auth).">
Log in to the [RazorpayX Dashboard](https://x.razorpay.com/auth).
</Step>
<Step title="Click on the download icon.">
Click on the download icon.

</Step>
<Step title="Select a period from the drop-down, or select custom dates for whic...">
Select a period from the drop-down, or select custom dates for which you require the report.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose the format of your report- `.xlsx` or `.csv`.">
Choose the format of your report- `.xlsx` or `.csv`.
</Step>
<Step title="You can **DOWNLOAD** as well as **EMAIL** the report. You can enter...">
You can **DOWNLOAD** as well as **EMAIL** the report. You can enter any email address you wish to share the report with as well as select the team members you want to share it with.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>

**Watch Out!**

Generating reports can take upto 10 minutes.
</Warning>
