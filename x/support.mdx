## RazorpayX Users

To raise a support ticket:

1. Log in to the [RazorpayX Dashboard](https://x.razorpay.com/auth).
1. You can raise a ticket in two ways: 

    
        
### From My Account & Settings

             1. Navigate to user profile icon at the top-right corner and click **My Account & Settings**.
             1. Select **Support Tickets Inbox** and click **+ NEW QUERY**.
             1. Select whether to: 
                - [**Raise a new request**](#raise-a-new-request). 
                - [**File a grievance**](#file-a-grievance).

                 Click **Next**.
                  
            

        
### Need Help Widget

             1. Click the **Need Help?** button at the bottom-right corner of the screen. 
             1. Click **Write To Us** in the bottom menu. You can also browse through the FAQs/help articles to resolve your queries.
             1. Select either of the following: 
                - [**Raise a new request**](#raise-a-new-request). 
                - [**File a grievance**](#file-a-grievance).

                 Click **Next**.
            

    

    

### Raise a New Request

You can raise a request when you wish for help in setting up certain processes, enabling features or to report an issue.

<Steps>
<Step title="Select the category of the issue from the drop-down menu.">
Select the category of the issue from the drop-down menu.
</Step>
<Step title="Write a description of the issue faced for our better understanding...">
Write a description of the issue faced for our better understanding and attach images if available.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter the email addresses of other users who you wish to keep infor...">
Enter the email addresses of other users who you wish to keep informed.
</Step>
<Step title="Click **Submit**.">
Click **Submit**.
</Step>
</Steps>

    

### File a Grievance

You can file a grievance when you are not satisfied with the support provided for a request you raised in the past.

<Steps>
<Step title="Select the reason for you to file a grievance.">
Select the reason for you to file a grievance.
</Step>
<Step title="Mention your ticket reference number.">
Mention your ticket reference number.
</Step>
<Step title="Click **Next**.">
Click **Next**.

</Step>
<Step title="Write a description of the complaint and expectations and click **S...">
Write a description of the complaint and expectations and click **Submit**.
</Step>
</Steps>

    

You have successfully raised a support request from the RazorpayX Dashboard.
