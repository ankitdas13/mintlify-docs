---
title: "Bulk Payouts Life Cycle"
description: "A payout created using the Bulk Upload feature can have the following statuses during its life cycle:"
---

- `pending`
- `scheduled`
- `processing`
- `processed`
- `reversed`
- `cancelled`
- `rejected`
- `failed`

Know more about [payout life cycle and statuses](/x/payouts/states-life-cycle).

<Info>
**Handy Tips**
</Info>

- Payouts created using the Bulk Upload feature are not queued. In case of insufficient balance, the payouts will fail.
- The `pending` and `rejected` statuses are available only if you have [Approval Workflow](/x/manage-teams/approval-workflow) enabled on your account.
