**Important Notice**

The automated GST payment feature is no longer available. If you're an existing user, you can access historical challans in the GST payments section on the Dashboard.

## View GST Challan

<Steps>
<Step title="Log in to your [RazorpayX Dashboard](https://x.razorpay.com/).">
Log in to your [RazorpayX Dashboard](https://x.razorpay.com/).
</Step>
<Step title="Navigate to **Menu** → **Tax Payments**.">
Navigate to **Menu** → **Tax Payments**.
</Step>
<Step title="Go to **GST Payments** tab.">
Go to **GST Payments** tab.
</Step>
<Step title="You can use the **VIEW CHALLAN** button to download the challan. Cl...">
You can use the **VIEW CHALLAN** button to download the challan. Clicking on **VIEW CHALLAN** redirects you to the GST portal.
</Step>
</Steps>
