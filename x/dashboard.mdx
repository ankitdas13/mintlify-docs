### Switch Merchant

To switch you merchant profile:

<Steps>
<Step title="Go to Profile.">
Go to Profile.
</Step>
<Step title="Click **Switch Merchant**.">
Click **Switch Merchant**.
</Step>
<Step title="Select the Merchant you want to switch to and wait for the page to...">
Select the Merchant you want to switch to and wait for the page to refresh.
</Step>
</Steps>

### My Account & Settings

Tab | Actions
---
User Profile | - To find and edit details like your username, password, phone number.
- To disable or enable 2 FA.

---
Business Profile | - To find business details like KYC status, business name and ID, website, tax details etc.
- To set expiry for Payout Links.

---
Public Profile | To customise Payout Links display screen with a colour or logo of your choice and support details.
---
Banking | To find all your affiliated bank account details.
---
[Manage Team](/x/manage-teams) | - To enable 2FA for the team.
- To add and assign roles to the team members.

---
Developer Controls | To [generate API keys](/x/dashboard/api-keys) and edit webhooks.
---
[Support Tickets](/x/support) Inbox | To raise queries in case of trouble and follow up on the existing ones.
---
Integrations | To [integrate with accounting tools](/x/accounting) for easier reconciliation.
---
