If you are a Zoho Books user, you can integrate your Zoho account with RazorpayX to sync payment information between RazorpayX to Zoho Books. This makes reconciliation easy.

## Integrate with Zoho Books
 
To start the integration process:
 
<Steps>
<Step title="Log in to your [RazorpayX Dashboard](https://x.razorpay.com/).">
Log in to your [RazorpayX Dashboard](https://x.razorpay.com/).
</Step>
<Step title="Navigate to **Profile** → **My Accounts & Settings** → **Integratio...">
Navigate to **Profile** → **My Accounts & Settings** → **Integrations**.
</Step>
<Step title="Select **ZOHOBOOKS** in the Accounting Tool Integration menu.">
Select **ZOHOBOOKS** in the Accounting Tool Integration menu.
</Step>
<Step title="You can **Invite Finance Team →** or integrate on your own by click...">
You can **Invite Finance Team →** or integrate on your own by clicking **Integrate Myself**. If you have invited your finance team, they can click **Integrate →**.
</Step>
<Step title="Select the domain in which your Zoho Books is hosted.">
Select the domain in which your Zoho Books is hosted.
1. Click **Proceed with .in** if your domain is .IN
2. Click **Proceed with .com** if your domain is .COM
</Step>
<Step title="Now, RazorpayX will redirect to the Zoho Books website. Login to yo...">
Now, RazorpayX will redirect to the Zoho Books website. Login to your Zoho Books account and click **Accept**.
</Step>
<Step title="You are then redirected back to RazorpayX to complete the final ste...">
You are then redirected back to RazorpayX to complete the final step. Select your organisation from the drop-down list which is synced from Zoho Books.
</Step>
<Step title="Your GSTIN is pre-filled. Click **NEXT**. This integration works on...">
Your GSTIN is pre-filled. Click **NEXT**. This integration works only if you have a GSTIN attached to your Zoho Books account.
</Step>
<Step title="Connect your RazorpayX bank account/s to an existing Zoho Books led...">
Connect your RazorpayX bank account/s to an existing Zoho Books ledger or create a new one by selecting from the drop-down menu. All payments made via the chosen RazorpayX bank account appear in the respective ledger on Zoho Books.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Accounting on RazorpayX Dashboard

After you integrate with Zoho Books, navigate to the Accounting tab from the [RazorpayX Dashboard](https://x.razorpay.com/). You can sync your payouts and vendor payments to Zoho Books here.

Payouts are listed under the **Expenses** tab and Vendor Payments are listed under the **Bills** tab.

## Rules & Configuration

The following are the available settings for your Zoho Books Integration:

   
### General

       You can choose to disable or enable the following options:

         - Sync RazorpayX Bank [Account Statement](/x/accounting/zohobooks/account-statement) to Zoho Books
         - Sync and Categorise [Vendor Payments](/x/accounting/zohobooks/vendor-payments) from RazorpayX to Zoho Books
         - Sync and Categorise [Payouts](/x/accounting/zohobooks/payouts) from RazorpayX to Zoho Books

       You can also **Refresh** Zoho Books Expense Accounts, Bank Ledgers and Tax Groups.
      

   
### Rules

       Setting rules will automate categorisation and hence reduce the manual effort drastically. You can set the following rules:
       - Contact Rules
         - Set contact rules for [categorisation of Vendor Payments](/x/accounting/zohobooks/vendor-payments#categorise).
         - Select the drop-down under Zoho Books Expense Account and choose the relevant Account for the particular contact. You can also **Add Contact +** and add a new rule for it.
         
       - Purpose Rules
         - Set purpose rules for [categorisation of Payouts](/x/accounting/zohobooks/payouts#categorise).
         - Select the drop-down under Zoho Books Expense Account and choose the relevant Account for the particular RazorpayX Purpose and select the relevant Tax Slab. You can also **Add Purpose +**.
         
      

   
### Contact Mapping

       Select the relevant Zoho Vendor from the drop-down menu or **+ Create New Vendor** to map it to the particular RazorpayX Contact to avoid duplication of vendors on Zoho Books.

         
      

   
### Account Mapping

       Setup ledgers in Zoho Books for RazorpayX accounts. Select the Zoho Books Expense Account you want to map with the particular RazorpayX Account.
