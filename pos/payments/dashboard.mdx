## View Payment Details

To view the details of a payment made to your account:
<Steps>
<Step title="Log in to your Dashboard.">
Log in to your Dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Click **Transactions** → **Payments**.">
Click **Transactions** → **Payments**.
</Step>
<Step title="Click **Details** for the selected Payment ID.">
Click **Details** for the selected Payment ID.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
**Handy Tips**
</Info>

Click on the **Charge Slip** file name to view it. Click the download icon next to the **Charge Slip** file name to save it on your system.

## View Settlement Details of a Payment

To view a detailed break-down of a settlement made to your account:
<Steps>
<Step title="Log in to your Dashboard.">
Log in to your Dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Navigate to **Transactions** → **Payments**.">
Navigate to **Transactions** → **Payments**.
</Step>
<Step title="Click on the specific **Payment Id** for which you want to view the...">
Click on the specific **Payment Id** for which you want to view the settlement details.
</Step>
<Step title="In the **Payment Details** view, you can view the settlement details.">
In the **Payment Details** view, you can view the settlement details.
</Step>
<Step title="Click on the **settlement_id** to view the detailed breakdown.">
Click on the **settlement_id** to view the detailed breakdown.
</Step>
</Steps>
