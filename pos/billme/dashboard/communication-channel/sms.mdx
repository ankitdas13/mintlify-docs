**Handy Tips**

TRAI regulations require you to link your SMS service provider's Telemarketer ID with your DLT account when using SMS for campaigns. Complete this step to ensure SMS delivery as messages will not be delivered without it.

## Overview

SMS Campaigns are ideal for high-speed, high-visibility communication. Use Billme to automate transactional or promotional SMS based on billing events or customer actions.

To access SMS campaigns page:

1. Select **SMS** under **Communication Channel** in the left navigation menu.
2. Alternatively, you can also select **SMS** in the **Select Campaign Listing** dropdown on the **All Campaigns** page.

## Create New SMS Campaign

To create a new SMS Campaign:

<Steps>
<Step title="Click **+ New SMS Campaign**.">
Click **+ New SMS Campaign**.
The **SMS - New Campaign** page appears.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter the Campaign Name and Description, select your Company and Co...">
Enter the Campaign Name and Description, select your Company and Content Type and click **Next Step**.
Content Type can be:
- Promotional: Select for SMS Campaigns with promotions and ads.
- Service Explicit: Select to communicate service information such as activation or expiry of a service.

</Step>
<Step title="Select or upload **Sender ID** and **Template**. Click **Next Step**.">
Select or upload **Sender ID** and **Template**. Click **Next Step**.

</Step>
<Step title="Enter the message and select the variable attributes if any. Click...">
Enter the message and select the variable attributes if any. Click **Next Step**.

</Step>
<Step title="Select **Whom To Send** on this page and click **Next Step**. You can:">
Select **Whom To Send** on this page and click **Next Step**. You can:
1. Select **Uploaded Data** and add/upload the list of receivers for the SMS Campaigns.
2. Select **Segmentation** and choose a specific customer segment for the SMS Campaigns. You can also add and select a new customer segment.

</Step>
<Step title="Select **When To Send** on this page. You have the option to select...">
Select **When To Send** on this page. You have the option to select **Send Now**, **Send Later** and **Recurring**. Configure the timelines and click **Next Step**.

</Step>
<Step title="Preview the SMS Campaign and test it on a valid phone number. You c...">
Preview the SMS Campaign and test it on a valid phone number. You can only test it once. Click **Publish** to publish the campaign.

The Published Campaign appears on the list of campaigns.
</Step>
</Steps>
