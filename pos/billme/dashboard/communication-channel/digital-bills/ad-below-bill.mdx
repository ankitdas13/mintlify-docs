## Create New Ad Below Bill Campaign

To create a new Ad Below Bill Campaign:

<Steps>
<Step title="Click **Ad Below Bill** under **Digital Bills**. The **Ad Below Bil...">
Click **Ad Below Bill** under **Digital Bills**. The **Ad Below Bill** page appears with the list of all Ad Below Bills campaigns.
</Step>
<Step title="Click **+ New Ad Below Bill Campaign**. The **Ad Below Bill - New C...">
Click **+ New Ad Below Bill Campaign**. The **Ad Below Bill - New Campaign** page appears.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter the **Campaign Details**, select company and click **Next Ste...">
Enter the **Campaign Details**, select company and click **Next Step**. The **Advert Builder** page appears.
- You can add ad components such as images, Carousels, GIFs, Videos or YouTube Videos.
- You can choose media directly from your recent uploads.
- You can also add any coupons on the ad.
</Step>
<Step title="Select an image for more options such as adding redirect url, surve...">
Select an image for more options such as adding redirect url, survey url or custom url. Click **Next Step**.
</Step>
<Step title="On the **Campaign Settings** page, select your **Brand** and **Stor...">
On the **Campaign Settings** page, select your **Brand** and **Stores**. You can also choose to publish the campaign immediately or schedule it. Click **Next Step**.
</Step>
<Step title="On the **Preview & Publish** page you can check all campaign detail...">
On the **Preview & Publish** page you can check all campaign details. Click **Export Report** and enter your email to opt for exporting campaign reports. Click **Publish**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Your **Ad Below Bill** Campaign is published and appears in the list of campaigns.
