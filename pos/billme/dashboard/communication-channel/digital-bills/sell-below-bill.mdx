## Create New Sell Below Bill Campaign

To create a new Sell Below Bill Campaign:

<Steps>
<Step title="Click **Sell Below Bill** under **Digital Bills**. The **Sell Below...">
Click **Sell Below Bill** under **Digital Bills**. The **Sell Below Bill** page appears with the list of all Sell Below Bill campaigns.
</Step>
<Step title="Click **+ New Sell Below Bill Campaign**. The **Sell Below Bill - N...">
Click **+ New Sell Below Bill Campaign**. The **Sell Below Bill - New Campaign** page appears.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter the **Campaign Details**, select company and click **Next Ste...">
Enter the **Campaign Details**, select company and click **Next Step**. The **PDF Upload** page appears.
- You can upload a PDF of your product catalogue to display below each invoice.

</Step>
<Step title="On the **Campaign Settings** page, select your **Brand** and **Stor...">
On the **Campaign Settings** page, select your **Brand** and **Stores**. You can also choose to publish the campaign immediately or schedule it. Click **Next Step**.
</Step>
<Step title="On the **Preview & Publish** page you can check all campaign detail...">
On the **Preview & Publish** page you can check all campaign details. Click **Export Report** and enter your email to opt for exporting campaign reports. Click **Publish**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Your **Sell Below Bill** Campaign is published and appears in the list of campaigns.
