## Create New Add Survey Button Campaign

To create a new Add Survey Button Campaign:

<Steps>
<Step title="Click **Add Survey Button** under **Digital Bills**.">
Click **Add Survey Button** under **Digital Bills**.
</Step>
<Step title="Click **+ New Add Survey Button Campaign**. The **Add Survey Button...">
Click **+ New Add Survey Button Campaign**. The **Add Survey Button - New Campaign** page appears.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter the **Campaign Details**, select company and click **Next Ste...">
Enter the **Campaign Details**, select company and click **Next Step**. The **Select Survey** page appears.
- You can search and select a previously created survey.
- You can create a survey from the **Survey** feature under **Create** section.

</Step>
<Step title="On the **Survey Button Builder** page, choose an image for your sur...">
On the **Survey Button Builder** page, choose an image for your survey button. You can also click **Create From Scratch** to upload a new button image. Click **Next Step**.
</Step>
<Step title="On the **After Survey** page, select an image or gif which appears...">
On the **After Survey** page, select an image or gif which appears right after survey completion. You can upload a new image. You can also add a **Coupon**. Click **Next Step**.
</Step>
<Step title="On the **Campaign Settings** page, select your **Brand** and **Stor...">
On the **Campaign Settings** page, select your **Brand** and **Stores**. You can also choose to publish the campaign immediately or schedule it. Click **Next Step**.
</Step>
<Step title="On the **Preview & Publish** page you can check all campaign detail...">
On the **Preview & Publish** page you can check all campaign details. Click **Export Report** and enter your email to opt for exporting campaign reports. Click **Publish**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Your **Add Survey Button** Campaign is published and appears in the list of campaigns.
