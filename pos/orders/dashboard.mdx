## View Order Details From Dashboard

To view order details:
<Steps>
<Step title="Log in to the Dashboard.">
Log in to the Dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Select **Transactions** from the left menu and go to the **Orders**...">
Select **Transactions** from the left menu and go to the **Orders** tab.
</Step>
<Step title="A list of all the orders is displayed.">
A list of all the orders is displayed.
</Step>
</Steps>

    

4. Click an **Order Id** to view the details of the order.
