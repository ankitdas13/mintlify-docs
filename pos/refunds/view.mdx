## View Details of a Refund

To view a refund:
<Steps>
<Step title="Log in to the Dashboard.">
Log in to the Dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Click **Transactions** on the left navigation and **View All** on t...">
Click **Transactions** on the left navigation and **View All** on the **Refunds** tab.
</Step>
<Step title="Click a **Refund Id** to view details of the refund.">
Click a **Refund Id** to view details of the refund.
</Step>
</Steps>

## View Settlement Details of a Refund

To view a detailed break-down of a settlement made to your account:
<Steps>
<Step title="Log in to the Dashboard.">
Log in to the Dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Navigate to **Transactions** → **Refunds**.">
Navigate to **Transactions** → **Refunds**.
</Step>
<Step title="Click the **Refund Id** for which you want to view the settlement d...">
Click the **Refund Id** for which you want to view the settlement details.
</Step>
<Step title="In the **Refund Details** view, you can see the settlement details.">
In the **Refund Details** view, you can see the settlement details.
</Step>
<Step title="Click the **settlement_id** to view a detailed breakdown.">
Click the **settlement_id** to view a detailed breakdown.
</Step>
</Steps>
