## Prerequisites

1. Sign up for a [Razorpay account](https://dashboard.razorpay.com/signup).
2. Sign up for a [Zapier account](https://zapier.com/).

## Integration Steps

Follow these steps to integrate your Zapier application with Razorpay:

<Steps>
<Step title="Click **Create Zap** on the top-left of the Zapier Dashboard page.">
Click **Create Zap** on the top-left of the Zapier Dashboard page.
</Step>
<Step title="Name your Zap.">
Name your Zap.
</Step>
<Step title="Search for the Razorpay option in the list of triggers.">
Search for the Razorpay option in the list of triggers.
</Step>
<Step title="Select an event based on your use case.">
Select an event based on your use case.
</Step>
<Step title="Click on **Continue**.">
Click on **Continue**.
</Step>
<Step title="Click **Sign in to Razorpay** to connect to your Razorpay account t...">
Click **Sign in to Razorpay** to connect to your Razorpay account through OAuth.
</Step>
<Step title="You will see this screen if you are logged in, or else you will be...">
You will see this screen if you are logged in, or else you will be prompted to log in.
</Step>
<Step title="Once authorised, you will be redirected back to the Zapier page. Yo...">
Once authorised, you will be redirected back to the Zapier page. You are now linked to your account.
</Step>
<Step title="Click **Continue** to get a sample test trigger.">
Click **Continue** to get a sample test trigger.
</Step>
</Steps>

    
<Info>
**Handy Tips**
</Info>

Test trigger is a sample payment that Zapier will pull from your account so that you can continue with the rest of your workflow.

10. To get a test trigger, you need to have at least one successfully captured payment from the recent 1000 payments.

You can repeat the process by choosing from a range of various apps.
