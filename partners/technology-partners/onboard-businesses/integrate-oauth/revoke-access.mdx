---
title: "Revoke Access to Application"
description: "Revoking access to a particular application restricts all permissions to access sub-merchant data and thereby removes it from the list of **Connected Applications**."
---

## Revoke Application Access From Dashboard

To revoke access to a connected application, the sub-merchant should follow these steps:

<Steps>
<Step title="Log in to the Dashboard.">
Log in to the Dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Navigate to **Applications** and click **Revoke** beside the applic...">
Navigate to **Applications** and click **Revoke** beside the application.

</Step>
<Step title="Confirm the action by clicking **Revoke Access**.">
Confirm the action by clicking **Revoke Access**.
</Step>
</Steps>
