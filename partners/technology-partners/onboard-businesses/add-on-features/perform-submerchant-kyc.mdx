**Feature Request**

This is an on-demand feature. Please raise a request with your Razorpay point-of-contact to get it activated on your Razorpay account.

#### Advantages

- **Decreases Onboarding Time by 50%** 

    Partner performing KYC for sub-merchants decreases onboarding time by half and ensures a seamless onboarding experience.
- **Value Added Service** 

    Partner performing KYC acts as a delight feature for your clients.

## Perform KYC

You can perform KYC for sub-merchants by following the steps given below:

<Steps>
<Step title="Log in to the Dashboard.">
Log in to the Dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Click **Partner** and navigate to **Affiliate Accounts**.">
Click **Partner** and navigate to **Affiliate Accounts**.
</Step>
<Step title="Click the **Account Name** of the sub-merchant for whom your want t...">
Click the **Account Name** of the sub-merchant for whom your want to perform KYC.
</Step>
<Step title="In the tab that opens, click **Perform KYC**.">
In the tab that opens, click **Perform KYC**.

</Step>
<Step title="Fill in the KYC details on the form and upload the necessary files.">
Fill in the KYC details on the form and upload the necessary files.

</Step>
</Steps>

You can view the activation status on the Partner Dashboard.

You can request to switch off KYC updates communications to sub-merchants.
