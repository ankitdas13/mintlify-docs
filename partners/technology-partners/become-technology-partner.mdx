All Partners are onboarded as Service Partners by default. You can request a switch if you have a use case for the Technology Partner type. Know how to become a [Razorpay Partner](/partners/new-to-razorpay).

## Request a Switch

To request a Partner type switch:

<Steps>
<Step title="Log in to the Dashboard.">
Log in to the Dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Navigate to **Partner** → **Home**.">
Navigate to **Partner** → **Home**.
</Step>
<Step title="Click **Explore Now**.">
Click **Explore Now**.

</Step>
<Step title="On the **Tell us more about the services you provide** page, select...">
On the **Tell us more about the services you provide** page, select the relevant business type and click **Next**.

</Step>
<Step title="If your business type is best suited for the Service Partner type,...">
If your business type is best suited for the Service Partner type, you will see the below screen.

</Step>
<Step title="If a different Partner type is more suitable for your business, you...">
If a different Partner type is more suitable for your business, you will see the below screen.

</Step>
<Step title="Select **Yes** if you have a product to manage payments for your su...">
Select **Yes** if you have a product to manage payments for your sub-merchants and click **Next**.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter your phone number, website URL, and business details, and cli...">
Enter your phone number, website URL, and business details, and click **Next**.

</Step>
</Steps>

You have successfully submitted a request for a Partner type switch. Our team will reach out to you with further instructions.

## Next Steps

If your switch request is approved, you will have to complete [full KYC](/partners/technology-partners/kyc) to start receiving commissions.

<Warning>

**Watch Out!**

Filling out the form does not guarantee a Partner type switch. Our team will evaluate your use case and contact you for more information. This process can take around 2 to 3 weeks.

</Warning>
