## Prerequisites

- Ensure you have a [Razorpay account](https://dashboard.razorpay.com/signup).
- Log in to the [Dashboard](https://dashboard.razorpay.com/) and complete the KYC requirements. Know more about [KYC Verification](/payments/set-up#2-submit-kyc-details).
- Generate [API Keys](/payments/dashboard/account-settings/api-keys#generate-api-keys) from the Dashboard. To go live, replace test keys with live keys.
- The Dashboard has [test and live modes](/payments/dashboard/test-live-modes). Use test mode for a sandbox experience before switching to live mode.
